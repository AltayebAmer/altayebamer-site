Born in Syria in 1971 and widely known in the art scene by the artistic name [ Altayeb Amer ]. A Syrian-born visual artist and multidisciplinary creative consultant based in Dubai, with more than three decades of professional experience in fine arts, Arabic calligraphy, and digital media. He has executed numerous creative projects for regional and international institutions and brands. His artworks are collected across the UAE, the Gulf region, and various Arab and international countries.
